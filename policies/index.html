Good to know

Client Policies

✦

These policies apply to every appointment with Two Generations Notary Solutions, LLC, from a single notarization to a full loan signing.

Booking & appointments+

Book by phone, text, email or the form on our website. We confirm every appointment in writing with the date, time, address and quoted total.

  • ✦Tell us when you book: the type of document, how many signers, how many signatures need notarizing, and whether witnesses are needed.
  • ✦Regular hours are 9 am to 5 pm, Monday through Friday. We are also available 24/7, including evenings, weekends, holidays and last-minute appointments, for an added fee listed on our Fees page.
  • ✦Same-day appointments depend on availability and carry a rush fee.
  • ✦For loan signings, the title company or signing service must send documents at least 2 hours before the appointment so we can print and review them.
Fees & payment+

Payment is due at the time of service. We accept cash, check, card, Zelle, Venmo and Cash App.

  • ✦Notary fees are $10 per notarial act, the most Alabama law allows. Travel and service fees are separate and always shown on their own line.
  • ✦We quote the full total before the appointment, and the price does not change unless the job changes (more signers, more stamps, longer wait).
  • ✦Title companies and signing services are invoiced and pay on the terms agreed when they book.
  • ✦For after-hours, same-day or long-distance appointments, we may ask for the travel fee upfront by payment link. This deposit is applied to your final total and is non-refundable once we are on the way.
  • ✦Returned checks carry a $25 fee.
Cancellations, rescheduling & waiting time+

You can cancel or reschedule for free any time before we leave for your appointment. Once we are on the way, the travel fee is due.

  • ✦Rescheduling is free with at least 2 hours’ notice.
  • ✦If no signer is there when we arrive, or the signing can’t go ahead because of missing ID or documents, the travel fee is due.
  • ✦The first 15 minutes of waiting are free. After that, waiting time is $10 per 15 minutes.
  • ✦If we are running late, we will call or text you before your appointment time.
What signers must bring+

Every signer must be present in person with a current, government-issued photo ID.

  • ✦Accepted ID includes a driver’s license, state ID card, passport or military ID. The name on the ID must match the name on the document.
  • ✦Leave documents unsigned until we are there, since we must watch you sign. Fill in all blanks before the appointment.
  • ✦If your document needs witnesses, tell us when you book. You can bring your own, or we can provide one for $15 each.
  • ✦If a signer has no valid ID, call us before booking so we can talk through options.
When we may decline a notarization+

We will not notarize if anything about the signing raises a legal or ethical concern. When that happens, fees already earned (such as travel) are still due.

  • ✦The signer can’t be identified, or the ID is expired, damaged or doesn’t match.
  • ✦The signer seems confused, unaware of what they are signing, or pressured by someone else.
  • ✦The document is incomplete, has blank spaces, or appears altered.
  • ✦The signer is not physically present.
  • ✦We are asked to backdate, change a date, or notarize a signature we did not see.
What we can’t do+

We are notaries, not attorneys, so we cannot give legal advice or tell you what to sign.

  • ✦We can’t explain what a document means legally, choose which form you need, or fill out legal documents for you.
  • ✦We can’t choose the type of notarial certificate for you. If your document has none, you must tell us which one you need.
  • ✦We don’t notarize our own signatures, each other’s, or a family member’s, or any document we have a personal or financial interest in.
  • ✦During loan signings we can point out where to sign and date, but loan terms and rates are questions for your lender.
Hospital & care facility visits+

We gladly come to hospitals, nursing homes, assisted living and private homes for signers who can’t travel. These visits carry a $15 added fee.

  • ✦Before we come, please check the facility’s visiting rules and let staff know we are coming.
  • ✦The signer must be awake, able to communicate, and understand what they are signing. If they can’t, we will reschedule rather than notarize.
  • ✦The signer must still have valid ID. If it has expired, call us first.
  • ✦We serve as notaries only during these visits and do not give medical opinions.
Privacy & document handling+

What you share with us stays confidential. We never sell or share your personal information.

  • ✦We don’t keep copies of your documents after the appointment, except as a title company or signing service requires.
  • ✦Loan documents we print are delivered as instructed, or shredded if left over.
  • ✦Emails and files you send us are deleted once they are no longer needed for the job.
Safety & conduct+

We want every appointment to be safe and respectful for everyone.

  • ✦We may end an appointment if we feel unsafe or if anyone is abusive. Fees earned up to that point are still due.
  • ✦Please secure pets before we arrive.
  • ✦If you prefer, we can meet at a public place, such as a library or coffee shop.
